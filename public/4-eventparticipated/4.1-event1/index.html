Event 1

EVENT REPORT: CLOUD ARCHITECT COMPETITION

Executive Summary & Overview

  • Event Title: Cloud Architect Competition
  • Date: June 20, 2026
  • Location: 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, HCMC
  • Format: Live Team-based AWS Cloud Architecture Competition
  • Role: Contestant – Team Leader
  • Team Name: Gặp phải thằng liều (Team #7)

Objectives & My Role as Team Leader

On June 20, 2026, I led our team “Gặp phải thằng liều” as Team Leader at the Cloud Architect Competition. This intense competition tested hands-on cloud skills, requiring participants to solve complex architecture challenges ranging from fundamental AWS infrastructure concepts to large-scale enterprise system design. Since our team hadn’t covered much of the advanced material yet, we decided to live up to our team name and take a bold, “reckless” leap with the exam questions.

As Team Leader, my responsibilities extended beyond answering questions. I actively facilitated live discussions among our 5 team members, evaluated risk trade-offs under tight time limits, and made the final calls when finalizing answers and triggering special game boosters.

Competition Format & Participating Teams

The tournament gathered 8 teams (5 members per team):

  1. KLKAT
  2. PrimeOps
  3. Team newbie
  4. Young Flame IT
  5. Loser
  6. Ngũ Đại Hiệp
  7. Gặp phải thằng liều (Our Team)
  8. Lifelong Learners

Tournament Format: Single-elimination bracket (Quarter-Finals ➔ Semi-Finals ➔ Finals). In the Quarter-Finals, our team Gặp phải thằng liều went head-to-head against team Lifelong Learners.

Question Difficulty & Point Breakdown:

  • AWS Cloud Practitioner (10 pts): Cloud fundamentals, IAM, AWS Global Infrastructure, Billing & Pricing, Well-Architected Framework.
  • AWS Solutions Architect – Associate (20 pts): EC2, S3, VPC, ELB, Auto Scaling, RDS, High Availability, Disaster Recovery.
  • AWS Solutions Architect – Professional (50 pts): Enterprise Architecture, Hybrid Cloud, Multi-Account, Migration, Cost Optimization, and Security Architecture.

Special Skill Boosters:

  • Minimum Risk: No penalty for wrong answers; correct answers earn 50% points.
  • Hope Star: Double points for correct answers; double penalty for incorrect answers.

Match Dynamics & Personal Reflection

During the match, leading our team under intense time constraints required balancing speed and analytical accuracy. We engaged in vigorous debate over questions demanding optimal trade-offs between cost efficiency and High Availability (HA).

Although our team fought hard and collaborated smoothly, our tournament run ended in a narrow knockout defeat, which left me feeling a bit of regret. With more preparation and better timing for our “Hope Star” booster, we could have advanced further in the bracket.

Nevertheless, it was an extraordinarily thrilling, fun, and memorable experience! Standing in the high-stakes arena at Bitexco Financial Tower, testing our AWS reflexes under pressure, and competing shoulder-to-shoulder with my team made it a highlight of our internship journey.

Technical Takeaways & Application to Our Project

Despite the match outcome, the competition provided invaluable architectural insights that directly benefit our team’s Vietnamese Corporate Financial Distress Analytics Platform:

  1. AWS Well-Architected Mindset: Evaluating solutions against the 5 pillars (Security, Reliability, Performance, Cost, Operational Excellence) rather than simply memorizing service names.
  2. Implementation in Our Financial Analytics System:
    • Designing VPC isolation (Public/Private Subnets) and strict Security Group rules for our calculation engines.
    • Optimizing S3 Data Lake storage and automating Glue ETL workflows with Step Functions.
    • Balancing Amazon Athena query speeds with serverless cost budgets.
  3. Leadership & Pressure Management: Enhancing active listening, decisive risk evaluation, and technical communication under tight deadlines.

Conclusion

The Cloud Architect Competition was both a high-stakes technical arena and an unforgettable milestone in my AWS internship journey. As Team Leader, the experiences, insights, and even the slight regret from our match serve as strong motivation for me to keep refining my cloud architecture expertise and leading our team to successfully deliver our AWS Financial Analytics Platform.
